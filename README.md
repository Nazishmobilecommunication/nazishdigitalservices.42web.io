# Nazish Digital Services

**Website:** https://nazishdigitalservices.42web.io

Welcome to **Nazish Digital Services**, your online destination for digital assistance and common online application services.

## About

Nazish Digital Services helps customers access digital services and get assistance with online applications and forms. Please contact the centre to confirm service availability, required documents, fees, and processing times before applying.

## Services

Services may include the following, where available and authorized:

- PAN card application and correction assistance
- Aadhaar-related correction guidance and other permitted Aadhaar services
- Online forms and application assistance
- Government portal and digital service assistance
- Printing, scanning, and document-related services

**Important:** Aadhaar services are subject to UIDAI rules and the centre's authorization. Never share your Aadhaar OTP, bank PIN, or passwords with anyone.

## How to Use the Website

1. Visit https://nazishdigitalservices.42web.io
2. Review the services and instructions provided on the website.
3. Contact the centre to confirm eligibility, documents, service charges, and processing time.
4. Use official government portals for applications and status checks whenever possible.

## Contact

For enquiries, please use the contact details published on the website.

## Privacy and Security

- Provide personal documents only when they are required for the requested service.
- Avoid sending sensitive information through public comments or unsecured channels.
- Keep application receipts and reference numbers safe.
- Use official government websites for final submissions and status checks.

## Disclaimer

Nazish Digital Services is an independent digital assistance service unless explicitly stated otherwise. This website is not an official government website and should not be represented as one. Government departments and service providers determine application approval, fees, and processing times. Service availability may change.
